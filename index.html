Temperance Downing

Temperance Downing (b. 2005) is a graphic designer based in New York City as well as a current BFA Communications Design student at Parsons School of Design. Her work has been exhibited at the Offset Bookfair and the Shanghai Unfold Bookfair.

Inspired by hardware tools and connection points, Wares is a typeface created in Glyphs that highlights the satisfying nature of tools and equipment. The specimen includes laser cut pieces that can be screwed together to create each letter.
